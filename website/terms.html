Description of Service

Voxboard is an iOS application and keyboard extension that provides on-device voice-to-text transcription. The app uses the whisper.cpp engine — a local implementation of OpenAI's Whisper speech recognition model — to convert spoken audio into text entirely on your device.

Core transcription runs on your device using Apple's Neural Engine and Metal GPU acceleration. Audio and transcripts are not sent to servers; privacy-safe onboarding analytics may be sent as described in the Privacy Policy.
