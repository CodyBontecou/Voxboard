Privacy
Policy

Effective: February 9, 2026 · Last updated: May 30, 2026

Overview

Voxboard is built on a simple principle: your voice and transcript data never leave your device. We collect only minimal, privacy-safe onboarding analytics so we can understand setup friction without seeing what you say or type.

This Privacy Policy describes how Voxboard ("we," "our," or "the app") handles information when you use our iOS application and keyboard extension. We are committed to transparency: audio, transcripts, keystrokes, exported file contents, and user-entered text stay on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

Voxboard does not collect, transmit, or have access to any of the following:

  • Audio recordings or voice data
  • Transcribed text or transcription history
  • Keystrokes, typed text, or keyboard input
  • Personal information such as your name, email, or phone number
  • Precise location data, contacts, photos, or other unrelated on-device data
  • File names, folder paths, template text, exported note contents, or model file paths
  • Advertising identifiers, raw IP address storage, or third-party tracking identifiers

Privacy-Safe Onboarding Analytics

Voxboard sends a small set of first-party onboarding events to help us understand where setup succeeds or fails. These events are intentionally coarse and are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Onboarding analytics may include:

  • An anonymous Voxboard install ID used only to count funnel progress
  • Event names such as onboarding started, microphone permission completed, model setup completed, paywall shown, purchase or restore outcome, and onboarding completed
  • App version, build number, platform, and experiment variant
  • Coarse setup state such as microphone permission status, model engine and size bucket, file export format/mode, free-minute usage buckets, and paywall context

These analytics never include audio, transcripts, dictated text, keystrokes, file/folder paths, template text, model file paths, names, email addresses, raw dates, raw IP addresses, or device names.

On-Device Processing

All voice-to-text transcription is performed entirely on your device using the whisper.cpp engine — a local implementation of OpenAI's Whisper model optimised for Apple Silicon. Audio captured by the microphone is processed in real-time by the on-device model and is never transmitted over a network connection.

Transcription results are stored locally on your device within the app's sandboxed container and shared App Group. This data is accessible only to the Voxboard app and its keyboard extension. No external service, server, or third party can access this data.

Keyboard Extension

Voxboard includes a custom keyboard extension that enables voice-to-text input across all apps on your device. Apple requires us to disclose the following about keyboard extensions:

  • Full Access is not required. Voxboard's keyboard extension does not request or require "Full Access." It operates without any network connectivity.
  • The keyboard extension does not log, store, or transmit any keystrokes or text input beyond the active transcription session.
  • The keyboard extension communicates with the main Voxboard app only through the shared App Group container — all on-device.

Microphone Usage

Voxboard requests microphone access to capture audio for voice transcription. Microphone audio is:

  • Processed locally by the whisper.cpp engine on your device
  • Never recorded to persistent storage as raw audio files
  • Never transmitted to any server, API, or third-party service
  • Discarded immediately after transcription is complete

Whisper Models

Voxboard ships with a bundled Base model and allows you to download additional Whisper models of varying sizes. These model files are downloaded from open-source distribution sources and stored locally on your device. No account or personal information is required to download models.

Third-Party Services

Voxboard does not integrate with third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash-reporting SDKs, or cross-app tracking services. Privacy-safe onboarding events are sent directly to a first-party Cloudflare Worker endpoint operated for Voxboard and stored in Cloudflare D1 with the strict allowlist described above.

Data Storage & Deletion

All data — including transcription history, downloaded models, and settings — is stored locally on your device. You can delete this data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual transcriptions from the history view within the app
  • Removing downloaded models from Settings
  • Uninstalling the Voxboard app, which removes all associated data from your device

Children's Privacy

Voxboard does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The privacy-safe onboarding analytics described above are not intended to identify or contact a child and never include audio, transcripts, keystrokes, or user-entered content.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
