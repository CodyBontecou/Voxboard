01 / How It Works

Three taps.
Zero friction.

01
Enable the keyboard

Install Voxboard and enable it as a system keyboard in iOS Settings. Takes 30 seconds. Works everywhere text input exists.

→
02
Open any app

Safari, Messages, Notes, Slack — any app with a text field. Switch to Voxboard by holding the globe key.

→
03
Tap mic. Speak.

Hit the microphone button. Speak naturally. Whisper transcribes your voice instantly on your device. No upload. No wait.
