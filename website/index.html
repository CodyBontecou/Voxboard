01 — How it works

Three steps.
Zero friction.

Voxboard replaces your iOS keyboard with a voice-first interface. Tap, speak, done. Works in every app.

01
Start listening
Open the app and tap Start Listening. The microphone activates in the background, ready when you are.
02
Switch to any app
Open Safari, Notes, Messages — anywhere with a text field. The Voxboard keyboard is always ready.
03
Tap the mic, speak
Hit the microphone button on the keyboard. Speak naturally. Your words appear as text — instantly, privately, on-device.
02 — Features

Built for the
real world.

🔒

Fully Private

All processing happens on your device. No audio is uploaded. No transcripts are shared. Your voice stays yours.

⌨️

Custom Keyboard

Works as a system-wide keyboard extension. Use voice-to-text in any app — Messages, Notes, Safari, Slack, anywhere.

🧠

whisper.cpp Engine

Powered by OpenAI's Whisper model, optimized for Apple Silicon with whisper.cpp. State-of-the-art accuracy, locally.

🌍

Auto Language

Automatic language detection across 99+ languages. Or lock to your preferred language for faster results.

📋

Transcript History

Every transcription is saved locally. Swipe up to browse, copy, or reference past recordings anytime.

🎛️

Choose Your Model

Pick from Tiny (75 MB) to Large v3 Turbo (1.6 GB). Balance speed and accuracy for your needs.
