Vox.md for Mac now records meetings with system audio and your microphone kept as separate stems, and on iPhone you can pick the app's language right in Settings.

New
• Capture meetings on Mac with clear separation between what you hear and what you say — each stem is preserved for transcription.
• Choose the app language in Settings on iPhone and iPad, without changing your device language.
• Add the new {location} token to entry templates to place capture location exactly where you want it.

Improved
• Transcription models already on your Mac are used in place — no more duplicate copies taking up disk space.
• Speaker labels stay consistent across all capture flows.

Fixed
• Captures always finish: text processing and voice enrichment now run under strict deadlines instead of stalling.
• Clearer, more consistent German throughout the app.
