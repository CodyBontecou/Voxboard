New in this update:
• Keyboard haptic feedback — taps now have tactile feedback by default, with a Settings toggle to turn it off.
• Long-press accent callouts — hold a key on Latin-script languages to access accented variants (à, é, ñ, ü, etc.).
• Localized status messages on the keyboard toolbar across all supported languages.
• Bug fix for in-app recordings that could trigger duplicate IPC responses.
