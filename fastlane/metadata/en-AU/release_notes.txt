Record voice notes from your Apple Watch, then let Voxboard sync them back to your iPhone when you reconnect.

New in this update:
• Apple Watch recording — start and stop local Watch voice notes without reaching for your phone.
• Watch queue sync — recordings stay saved on Apple Watch and appear in your iPhone queue when the devices reconnect.
• Clearer status — redesigned Ready, Recording, Syncing, Queued, and Sent states make it easier to trust what is happening.
