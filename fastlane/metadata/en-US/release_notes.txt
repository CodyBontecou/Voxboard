Voxes are here: create named dictation presets for journaling, meetings, tasks, or any custom workflow, then route each one to its own format, folder, template, and audio rules.

New in this update:
• Lock Screen settings — turn the Live Activity/Dynamic Island monitor and Quick Record button on or off.
• Smarter note exports — each Vox can control Markdown, YAML, templates, filenames, frontmatter, and audio attachments.
• Safer migration — existing export settings move into your default Vox, with clearer post-processing choices and more reliable audio export.
