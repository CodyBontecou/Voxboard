This update adds smarter review timing and Mac workflow improvements.

New in this update:
• App Store reviews — Voxboard now asks for feedback only after you’ve had time to use it: after five successful transcriptions across at least two days.
• Mac global keybind — start recording from anywhere on macOS, then press the same shortcut to stop and transcribe.
• Mac Vox icons — choose custom Vox symbols with a searchable icon picker instead of typing SF Symbol names.
